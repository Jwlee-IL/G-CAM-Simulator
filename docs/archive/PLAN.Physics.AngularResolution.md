# PLAN.Physics.AngularResolution — angular resolution at use distance against the cell / D baseline

Scope: TODO-34 / decision D-41 (performance-critical). EV-11 / PR-IMG-04 say MLEM resolves source pairs **2–3 mm**
apart that cross-correlation merges — measured at the lab geometry with the source **100 mm** from the mask (near
field), on floods generated by the same binary pixel-centre model MLEM inverts (no model mismatch), cyclic decoding, a
truth-centred valley metric and 1.5 × 10⁶ photons. The product is used at metres. D-41: state spatial resolution as an
**angle at the use distance**, against a declared baseline — the head's geometric resolution cell / D (hand-held
1 mm / 55 mm → 1.042°) with cross-correlation — and report what a reconstruction gains as **two-source angular
separation** at field distance, under a stated resolved-pair criterion and stated counts. Until then EV-11 is a lab
near-field result only.

## Questions

- **Q1 — point response.** At 1 m and 5 m, what is the measured angular FWHM of a single source for cross-correlation
  (with and without sub-cell interpolation) and for MLEM, against the geometric 1.042°? This is the baseline actually
  delivered, not the formula.
- **Q2 — two-source separation.** The smallest angular separation at which a pair is **resolved** (criterion below)
  in ≥ 95 % of acquisitions, for cross-correlation and MLEM, versus detected counts, intensity ratio (1 : 1 and 1 : 4)
  and position in the field (on axis, near the fully-coded edge).
- **Q3 — what the old claim was worth.** Re-run the EV-11 recipe with the new criterion and with transported floods
  at the lab geometry, so the change from "2–3 mm" can be attributed (near field, model mismatch, cyclic, criterion).

## Decisions after review (2026-10-06)

The review ([PLAN.Physics.AngularResolution.Review](PLAN.Physics.AngularResolution.Review.md)) found six wrong premises
and made eleven proposals; the planner adopts all eleven. **Correction to "What exists":** `MlemStudy`'s floods sample
aim points over the pixel **area** (binary mask, no slab), not pixel centres (`MlemStudy.TwoSourceFlood`, checked by the
planner); `AmbientSweepStudy` runs at the scenario's own distance (S = 100 mm for the hand-held head), not at 1 m;
`MlemDecoder` takes no background term. The three product questions went to the author, who chose the recommended
option each time.

| ID | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DR-1 | **Q1:** no sub-cell variant (interpolation does not change an FWHM); the cross-correlation FWHM reported as a **distribution over source position within one element** (x and y, degrees); MLEM width only noiseless, labelled so | review #2 |
| DR-2 | **Resolved-pair criterion (author: v = 0.25, false split ≤ 5 %):** two peaks by **topographic prominence** above a baseline (image median for cross-correlation, 0 for MLEM), each assigned to a different true source within min(Δ / 2, 1 element); valley depth ≥ v = 0.25 of the lower peak (v = 0.5 as a sensitivity); every pass rate reported with the **single-source false-split rate** at the same counts; "resolved at Δ" = ≥ 95 % pass, false split ≤ 5 %, and every larger Δ on the grid also passes | review #3; **author** (Q3) |
| DR-3 | **Sampling phase:** pair position randomised by ±0.5 element and orientation alternated (x / y) per seed; 128 seeds × 50 repeats; intervals bootstrapped over seeds | review #4 |
| DR-4 | **Field (author):** resolution is **claimed inside the fully-coded field (±3.6°)**; both sources stay inside it for "near the edge"; one wider-grid condition in the usable non-cyclic field (±7°) is measured and reported, not claimed; a 5 m slice kept | review #5; **author** (Q2) |
| DR-5 | **MLEM iterations:** chosen on separate seeds by the smallest resolved separation *including* the false-split limit (not the raw pass rate), then frozen; sensitivity versus iterations at one condition | review #6 |
| DR-6 | **Three MLEM variants:** the engine's (pixel-centre), an **analytic pixel-area** system matrix (new, opt-in in `MlemDecoder`; the existing default must stay bit-identical so EV-01 / EV-34 / EV-11 numbers do not move), and a transported-map matrix as the matched upper bound. **PR-IMG-04 is claimed with the pixel-area MLEM (author)** | review #7; **author** (Q1) |
| DR-7 | **Ambient:** an optional background term b_i in `MlemDecoder` (default off, bit-identical), one ambient condition at 0.10 µSv/h (front-only and bare) | review #8 |
| DR-8 | **Q3 attribution ladder** at the lab geometry, one change per step (EV-11 recipe → blind criterion → transported floods → non-cyclic → 1 m), so the move from "2–3 mm" is attributed step by step | review #9 |
| DR-9 | **PR-IMG-02 flag:** its 0.25 mm floor comes from S = 100 mm; at 1 m the noiseless cross-correlation peak is off by up to 0.44 element — measured here as a by-product (single-source bias versus position) and flagged for the planner | review #10 |
| DR-10 | **Stated limits:** ideal pixel identification, homogeneous crystal, no inter-pixel dead regions (before the four-channel readout, TODO-19); still head (no hand motion) | review #11 |
| DR-11 | Corrections to "What exists" as above; "Not here" changes: the pixel-area MLEM *is* built here (as an option and the claimed reconstruction); a transported MLEM stays an upper bound only | review #1, #7 |
